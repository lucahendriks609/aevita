# Tone of voice

Warm, human, never shaming. Dutch, informal **je/jij**, sentence case
everywhere. Aevita normalises that willpower alone often fails and frames
medical support as relief — not failure.

## Do

- "Doe de geschiktheidscheck" — action-first CTAs
- "Jouw vaste verpleegkundige" — personal, possessive
- Reassure next to every CTA: "vrijblijvend", "gratis opzegbaar"
- Specific proof: "tot 22,5% gewichtsverlies"
- Light emoji inside the app only: "Lekker bezig, Els! ✨"

## Don't

- Title Case Sentences Like This
- Clinical distance ("de patiënt dient…")
- Shame or willpower framing
- Emoji on the marketing site or in medical copy
- Vague hype without numbers or a named person
