# Colour

Two heroes, one gradient. Mindful Mint drives action; Boost Blue carries the
brand and headings. Dynamic Dark is text-only.

## Primaries

- **Mindful Mint** `#00BC8E` — action / accent (primary CTA fill)
- **Boost Blue** `#3753F0` — brand / headings

## Blue family

- **Boost Blue** `#3753F0`
- **Balance Blue** `#338EF0`
- **Brave Blue** `#97C7F7`
- **Lovely Lilac** `#BDC2FF` — accent, skews toward the female audience

## Neutrals & text

- **Dynamic Dark** `#002327` — text only, never a fill for large surfaces
- **Muted** `#4A6B70`
- **Wise White** `#F2F7F8`
- **Cream** `#FAFFFE` — page background
- **Coral** `#FF6B47` — website-only accent, not in the brandbook; use sparingly

## Gradients

- **Signature** — `135deg, #00BC8E 0%, #3753F0 100%` — the site's standard mint→blue wash
- **Brandbook** — `-60deg, #BDC2FF 0%, #338EF0 45%, #00BC8E 100%` — the brandbook's own angle
- **Soft** / **Warm** / **Hero** — lighter tinted washes for section backgrounds

## Rules

- All blues combine freely.
- Never pair Lovely Lilac with Mindful Mint.
- Shadows are blue-tinted (`rgba(55,83,240, α)`), not grey — this is what makes
  Aevita surfaces feel light instead of heavy. See `--shadow-*` tokens.
